# AI-generation indicators (`synthetic`) — weak by design

**Decision (D14):** no ONNX classifier ships. Openly licensed AI-image detectors we know of are trained on narrow datasets (e.g. CIFAKE, single Stable-Diffusion versions) and fail on newer generators, post-processing and resizing; several are non-commercial or unlicensed. Shipping one would produce confident-looking but unreliable verdicts, which violates the project's principles.

**What the module checks** (always *low* severity, confidence ≤ 0.45):
- `synthetic.spectral_peaks`: peaks at N/4 or N/8 in the spectrum of the noise residual (3×3 median high-pass), compared with a ring around each candidate; only for lossless files (PNG/BMP/TIFF) because JPEG's grid creates the same peaks. Period 2 is ignored because Bayer demosaicing in genuine camera images produces it.
- `synthetic.canvas_size`: both sides are generator-typical multiples of 64 (512…2048) and there is no EXIF.
- An *info* finding always states the scope of the check.

**Much stronger evidence** comes from the metadata modules: C2PA `digitalSourceType`, IPTC `DigitalSourceType=trainedAlgorithmicMedia`, Stable Diffusion/ComfyUI/Midjourney parameters, AI tool names in Software/CreatorTool.

**References.** R. Corvi et al., "On the detection of synthetic images generated by diffusion models", ICASSP 2023 (spectral artefacts); X. Zhang, S. Karaman, S.-F. Chang, "Detecting and simulating artifacts in GAN fake images", WIFS 2019.
